ContractDescent = run · Death = prestige

Until they die, you get nothing

A roguelike run and an incremental prestige are the same loop here. No gold, no Echo, no items until the contract closes. So coming back isn’t a habit, it’s an event: a notification arrives, and it’s time to see what they brought.

  1. 01

    Contract

    Hire a mercenary at the Tavern: rank, trait, nine gear slots, four skills and a standing order for forks.

  2. 02

    Descent

    Three waves and a chest per floor, a boss on every fifth. The fight runs without you — watch the log or show up at a fork.

  3. 03

    Death

    A notification arrives. The backpack, gold and Echo wait at the Outpost. If a relief waits by the Campfire, the next one is already heading down.

  4. 04

    Debrief

    Look over the finds, cut shards at the Forge, spend Echo in the tree — and build the next one.

↺ And back to the Tavern. The rope lowers the next one straight to 15% of your record.

Floor 5Bestiary · a boss every fifth floor

Twenty-three dwellers of the abyss

Five damage types, and every enemy has its own resistances and weaknesses. A build that ignores them pays in depth. The figures are drawn in code — the same ones as in the game’s arena — and all of them face left, toward the mercenary.

9 slotsBuild · 25 relics

A relic changes a rule, not a number

Two hands, a helmet, body armor, gloves, boots, two rings and an amulet. Items drop rarely, and each one is worth a look. A relic isn’t a bonus: it’s a new law of combat, and you pay for it. Pick a slot.

Eight relics are an address, not a find: only their own boss drops them.

55skills

Four slots to put them in. A skill scales either from the weapon or from spell power — two branches, and gear for each is built differently.

38properties

A property stores not only its value but the quality of its roll. Break an item into a shard and imprint it into another — a good roll never goes stale.

5damage types

Physical and four elements. A Conduit turns all your damage into one — and the boss who drops it resists that very element.

Floor 40Between descents · Brand of the Abyss

The Outpost grows on other people’s depth

Eight buildings, eight levels each. They cost gold, but each next level unlocks only with depth reached — you can’t buy it early.

Tavern

More candidates, and higher ranks among them. From level four — a second mercenary in the rift.

Armory

More loot on each descent, and better loot.

Forge

Reroll properties and deepen relics. Each level raises the lowest roll a reroll can give.

Vault

Room in the Outpost chest — for everything the fallen carried back.

Shard Bench

Keeps property shards, so a good roll can go into a new item.

Altar

The rate at which surplus is broken down into gold and shards.

Cartographer

Floor forecast: see which modifiers lie ahead — up to eleven floors.

Campfire

Rest between floors, and places in the relief: a replacement heads down the same second, in the same build.

Brand of the Abyss

Strength won’t decide it. What you bring will

A guardian is the same boss you meet in the abyss, but in its own lair and at full strength. No descent before it — only the duel. It winds up its skills, it has its own weakness, a challenge costs an offering, and a mercenary who loses dies.
